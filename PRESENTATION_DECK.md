## 💡 Presenter Tips & Presentation Q&A Cheat Sheet

### Common Evaluator Questions & Winning Answers:

1. **Q: How do you guarantee the AI doesn't hallucinate wrong numbers?**
   - **Answer**: *"We decoupled the neural language model from arithmetic calculation entirely. The LLM acts purely as an intent router and text synthesizer. All mathematical operations are performed by compiled Python functions using NumPy and statsmodels. Furthermore, an independent Audit Verifier node checks that the output strictly adheres to formula constraints before displaying it to the user."*

2. **Q: How is the domestic vs. import parity calculated?**
   - **Answer**: *"For domestic parity, we take the base mandi price, add the 1.5% Mandi Tax, loading expenses, 5% GST, and logistics freight to the plant. For import CIF, we take FOB Santos/Paranaguá prices, add ocean freight and marine insurance, convert at prevailing USD/INR FX, apply Basic Customs Duty (BCD), Social Welfare Surcharges, port CHA clearing, and inland rail/road haulage. This gives an exact apples-to-apples comparison in Rupees per quintal."*

3. **Q: Why use the CBOT 10:11:9 ratio?**
   - **Answer**: *"The 10:11:9 ratio is the global standard benchmark for soybean crushing. 10 contracts of soybeans (50,000 bushels) yield approximately 11 contracts of soybean meal (1,100 short tons at 44 lbs/bushel yield) and 9 contracts of soybean oil (540,000 lbs at 11 lbs/bushel yield). This allows commercial crushers to calculate board crush margins and hedge their input-output price spread simultaneously."*

4. **Q: How does the dynamic Reorder Point (ROP) prevent stockouts?**
   - **Answer**: *"Traditional static buffers use fixed quantities. Our system uses statistical safety stock calculated as `Z × σ_LT × d`, where Z is the service level factor (1.65 for 95% confidence), `σ_LT` is the standard deviation of supplier lead time, and `d` is the crushing plant's daily burn rate. This dynamically expands safety buffers during volatile weather or harvest delays and compresses them during stable periods to optimize working capital."*
